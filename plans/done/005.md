## Decisions

- 2026-10-16 — CI perf uses separate headless thresholds (`headless_frame.*` in budgets.json) rather than real-device budgets. Headless numbers are ~2× worse due to software rasterization; relative regression detection is the goal in CI, not absolute truth.

## Open risks

- GPU probe (timed draw) on mobile CI may be unreliable — if the probe misfires, fall back to `reduced` (safe side). Need to validate probe timing with a short timeout.
- `deviceMemory` API not available in Firefox or Safari — treat absence as neutral (don't downgrade tier on absence alone).
