## Audience
## Audience

### 1. Senior designers / design leaders (Staff+, Directors, VPs)
Often the person whose "yes" carries the loop. Skeptical, pattern-matching for craft and thinking.

**10 seconds:** Is this person at my level or above? Do they have taste?
- Hero line: specific problem, real scale, no buzzwords
- Visual quality of the site itself is the first proof of craft
- Problem domains that are hard (context engines, ML analytics, observability)

**5 minutes:** How do they think?
- The reasoning behind decisions: constraints, tradeoffs, what got cut and why
- Interaction models and systems, not just polished screens
- One original point of view on AI UX (e.g., context as the core design problem)
- Evidence they shape strategy, not just execute it

### 2. Design hiring managers / design recruiters
Screening for level and fit. Moving fast, comparing against a stack of candidates.

**10 seconds:** What level, what domain, can I put this person forward?
- Title (Senior Staff) and years (25+) visible immediately
- Recognizable names: Adobe, Snowflake, ServiceNow acquisitions
- AI/agentic as the current specialty, stated plainly
- Résumé + LinkedIn one click away

**5 minutes:** Will this person hold up in the loop?
- Scannable outcomes per case study (metrics in the first screen of each)
- Scope signals: team size, leadership, cross-org influence
- Range: 0→1 (Korl), leadership (Sisu), scale (Adobe), craft (Lightstep)
- Teammate quotes as third-party validation

### 3. Founders
Hiring a first or founding designer, or a design leader for an early team. Care about leverage and speed.

**10 seconds:** Can this person build the thing with us, from nothing?
- Korl front and center as proof: employee #1, $5M seed, 80% demo-to-second-meeting
- Technical fluency: the site itself was built with React + agents
- Ownership signal (skydiving story, founding roles)

**5 minutes:** Will they move fast without hand-holding and help us win customers?
- How research turned into strategy that helped raise money
- Shipping speed and design-system-for-velocity thinking
- Comfort with ambiguity, sales, and customer conversations
- Experience through acquisition: knows what "winning" looks like

## Implications for the site
- **First screen serves all three:** hero line + level + names. Everyone's 60-second test passes or fails here.
- **Case study cards = recruiter layer:** role, one-line problem, 2–3 metrics. No clicking required to qualify you.
- **Case study pages = designer/founder layer:** thinking, tradeoffs, systems. Open each with a 3-line summary so skimmers get the result before the process.
- **Korl does double duty:** it's the founder proof and the AI proof. Give it the richest case study.
- **The site is a case study:** a short "how this site was built" note (agent harness, design.md, tokens) is proof for senior designers and founders alike.

## Tone
Crisp, accurate, to the point. Not overly verbose. not generic wording every mid-level designer users. Never use "empathy, craft.
